Details of the dataset:

The dataset is describing the various species of penguins found in
various islands, their bill length, bill depth, flipper length, body
mass and sex.

The number of different species available in this dataset is 3. They
are: Adelie, Gentoo, Chinstrap.

The number of rows in the dataset is 344 and the number of columns is 8.

The mean flipper length is 201 mm.

Scatterplot of flipper length vs bill length:

